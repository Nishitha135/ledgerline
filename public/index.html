Move money. Keep the proof.

Deposits, withdrawals and transfers where the books always balance, a repeated request never pays twice, and every entry stays on the record.

  • Send a request again and you get the first answer back, not a second payment.
  • The server decides who may move money, on every request.
  • Every posting and every refusal is kept in a ledger that cannot be quietly edited.
